Before you connect.

How do I get started?

Sign in with GitHub, create a project, and connect a source. Add and verify a Slack or Discord destination, then choose which updates it receives.

Do I need to change my app?

Most sources connect through authorization or credentials. Better Auth needs a server connector. PostHog and Google Analytics use your existing instrumentation.

How soon do updates arrive?

It depends on the source: signed webhooks, scheduled checks, or daily reports. Store reports can arrive days later. This isn’t a real-time guarantee.

Can I self-host it?

Yes. The deployment guide covers Docker and Cloudflare. Email and Telegram are also supported when configured by the installation’s operator.
